# 🚀 BareMind Platform Feature Roadmap

A comprehensive guide of feature ideas and roadmap enhancements for the BareMind platform.

---

## ✍️ 1. Content Creation & Editor Enhancements

- **Auto-Save Drafts & Revision History:** Periodically save draft changes in local storage or backend database, allowing authors to view or restore previous versions of their posts.
- **Floating Table of Contents:** Automatically generate an interactive outline sidebar based on headings (`H1`, `H2`, `H3`) in long articles.
- **Embedded Rich Media:** Native support for embedding YouTube videos, CodePen embeds, X (Twitter) posts, and GitHub Gists seamlessly inside the editor.
- **AI Reading Time & Summary Generator:** Automatically generate a 2-sentence TL;DR summary at the top of long posts.
- **Scheduled Publishing:** Allow authors to pick a future date and time for a story to go live automatically.

---

## 💬 2. Reader Engagement & Social Features

- **Bookmarks & Reading List:** A "Save for Later" page where readers can curate their personal library.
- **Inline Text Highlighting & Comments:** Allow readers to select any text excerpt in an article to highlight it or leave an inline comment.
- **Multi-Reaction System:** Expand beyond standard likes to include specific reactions (e.g. 👏 *Clap*, 💡 *Insightful*, 🔥 *Fire*, ❤️ *Love*).
- **Email Newsletter / Author Subscriptions:** Allow readers to subscribe to their favorite authors and receive an email notification when a new blog is published.

---

## 📊 3. Analytics & Author Dashboard

- **Deep Story Analytics:**
  - View counts vs. Read completion rate (% of readers who reached the bottom).
  - Average time spent per article.
  - Referral traffic sources (Direct, Search, Social).
- **Author Stats Overview:** Total views, monthly growth charts, and top-performing articles.

---

## 🔍 4. SEO & Publishing Control

- **Custom SEO Metadata Editor:** Allow authors to set custom Open Graph preview images, Meta Titles, and Meta Descriptions.
- **Canonical URL Support:** Enable authors to set a canonical URL when cross-posting content from Medium, Dev.to, or a personal domain.
- **Custom Slugs:** Edit URL handles manually instead of strictly auto-generating them from the title.

---

## 🎨 5. Reader Experience & UI/UX

- **Reading Modes / Themes:** Toggle between Light Mode, Dark Mode, Sepia (warm paper feel), and OLED pitch black.
- **Text-to-Speech (Audio Reader):** An audio player at the top of the post so users can listen to articles on the go.
- **Advanced Search & Filtering:** Filter articles by tag, category, estimated read time (e.g., *Under 5 min*), and publication date.
